Prompt to Pan

The cooking side of planning meals with AI. Brainstorm a dinner with Claude, ChatGPT or Grok, then cook from its recipes, shopping list and timeline.

  • Plan it in a chatChat in the app with your own API key. When the plan is right, tap Send to pan and the meal lands in your collection. Or use the AI's own app and bring the meal over with a tap.
  • Cook from your phoneRecipes with every step and time, a shopping list that combines the whole meal, and a timeline that works back from when you want to eat.
  • Private by designNo account, no ads, no tracking. Your meals stay on your phone, and chat messages go straight to the AI you connect.
