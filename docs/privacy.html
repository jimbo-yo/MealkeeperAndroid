Prompt to Pan privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Prompt to Pan is a cooking app: you plan a meal with an AI assistant such as Claude, ChatGPT or Grok, bring it into the app, and cook from its recipes, shopping list and timeline. This policy explains what happens to your information.

The short version

Prompt to Pan has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and no server of its own: the developer never receives your information. Your meals stay on your phone. The app uses the internet only if you connect an AI with your own API key, and then what you write in the chat goes straight from your phone to that AI provider.

What stays on your phone

Your meals, recipes, shopping-list check marks and settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include them in your device backup to your Google account, which you control in your phone's settings.

Your clipboard

When you come back to the app after copying something, Prompt to Pan may read the new clipboard text to check whether it's a meal exported by your AI assistant, so it can offer to import it. It only looks at text copied since it last checked, skips content Android marks as sensitive, and never sends the clipboard anywhere. You can turn this off under Settings.

Chatting with your AI in the app

If you add an API key from Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT) or xAI (Grok) under Settings → AI accounts, you can chat with that AI inside Prompt to Pan. Your messages, and any meal or recipe you ask about, are sent over an encrypted connection directly from your phone to that provider, under your own account. The provider's privacy policy and terms cover what it does with them: Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI.

Your API keys are stored on your phone, encrypted with a key that never leaves the device, and are not included in Android backups. The chat itself is saved on your phone with your meals. Removing a key in Settings deletes it from the phone.

Tapping Report under a reply opens your email app with that reply filled in, addressed to prompttopan.support@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you send the email. Reports are used only to look into the problem.

When you send something to an AI app

Without an API key, tapping Revise with AI or Ask AI hands that meal or question to the ChatGPT, Claude or Grok app (or opens its website) so you can send it. Prompt to Pan only does this when you tap, and what happens next is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

Sharing meals and backups

When you share a meal or save a backup, the app creates a file and passes it to the app or location you choose through Android's share sheet or file picker. Nothing is uploaded by Prompt to Pan itself.

Children

Prompt to Pan is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions are welcome at prompttopan.support@gmail.com or on the project's GitHub issues page.
