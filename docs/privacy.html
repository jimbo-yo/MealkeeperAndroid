Prompt to Pan privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Prompt to Pan is a cooking app: you plan a meal with an AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude, bring it into the app, and cook from its recipes, shopping list and timeline. This policy explains what happens to your information.

The short version

Prompt to Pan does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't even have permission to use the internet. Your meals stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your meals, recipes, shopping-list check marks and settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include them in your device backup to your Google account, which you control in your phone's settings.

Your clipboard

When you come back to the app after copying something, Prompt to Pan may read the new clipboard text to check whether it's a meal exported by your AI assistant, so it can offer to import it. It only looks at text copied since it last checked, skips content Android marks as sensitive, and never sends the clipboard anywhere. You can turn this off under Settings.

When you send something to an AI app

When you tap Revise with AI or Ask AI, Prompt to Pan hands that meal or question to the ChatGPT or Claude app (or opens its website) so you can send it. Prompt to Pan only does this when you tap, and what happens next is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

Sharing meals and backups

When you share a meal or save a backup, the app creates a file and passes it to the app or location you choose through Android's share sheet or file picker. Nothing is uploaded by Prompt to Pan itself.

Children

Prompt to Pan is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions are welcome on the project's GitHub issues page.
